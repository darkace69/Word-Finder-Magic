# Privacy Policy — Word Finder Magic

**Last updated:** October 15, 2026

Word Finder Magic ("the App") is published by MAWiener. This policy explains what
information the App handles. In short: **the App does not collect, transmit, or
share your personal information.**

## Information We Collect

**None.** The App:

- Does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or any
  contact information.
- Does not include advertising SDKs, analytics, or crash-reporting tools.
- Does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.
- Does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Information Stored on Your Device

Game data — such as puzzles played, stars earned, grade level, settings, and any
custom themes or word lists you create — is stored **locally on your device**
only. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party. Deleting the App removes
this data.

## In-App Purchases

The App offers optional one-time purchases and subscriptions ("Premium") to
unlock unlimited puzzles. All payments are processed **entirely by Google Play**.
We do not see, collect, or store your payment card details. Google's handling
of purchase information is governed by the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children's Privacy

Word Finder Magic is designed for children (Pre-K through 5th grade) to use
with a parent or guardian. Because the App collects no personal information, we
do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a
child has provided personal information through the App, contact us at the email
below and we will address it promptly.

## Data Security

Since no personal information leaves your device, there is no server-side data
to protect. The limited purchase information handled by Google Play is protected
under Google's security practices.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a
revised "Last updated" date.
